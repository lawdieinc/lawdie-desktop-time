# Lawdie brand implementation

Authority: `lawdie/Lawdie Brand Guidelines (1)/Lawdie Brand Guidelines (1).docx`,
version 1.0 (2026), and the logo assets in that folder. These are the source of
truth for this app; the initial provisional green theme has been replaced.

- Cream `#F9F6F2` background, cream warm `#F3F0E8` navigation, translucent white cards.
- Espresso `#3D3731` text; muted taupe `#7C7366` secondary text; sand `#D8D1C7` dividers.
- Brown-gold `#8B5C2A` primary controls; warm brown and approved taupe shades for projects.
- Playfair Display 600 headings; Source Sans Pro regular/semibold interface;
  Fragment Mono uppercase tracked labels. System monospaced numerals remain for
  timers and durations to prevent layout shifts, and for paths/bundle identifiers.
- The supplied black Lawdie wordmark is used without recoloring or distortion.
  The supplied icon-only bird mark is resized proportionally into the macOS app icon.
- Frosted panels over warm cream fields, restrained highlights, 15-second ambient
  gradient movement and 0.34-second page transitions. Reduce Motion and Reduce
  Transparency are respected. Logo assets receive no effects or gradients.
- Sentence-case plain-English copy, with uppercase reserved for tracked labels.

The application bundles fonts locally; it never calls Google Fonts at runtime.
Font licenses accompany the resources. Sources:

- [Playfair Display](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/playfairdisplay)
- [Fragment Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fragmentmono)
- [Source Sans Pro 2.045](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans/tree/2.045R-ro%2F1.095R-it)

Lawdie logos are brand assets, not third-party open-source artwork.
